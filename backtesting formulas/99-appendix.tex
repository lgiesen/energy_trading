This section documents the use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools in accordance with good academic practice and transparency requirements. AI systems were used as auxiliary tools for language support and structured literature exploration; they were not used as autonomous scientific authors, and no AI-generated output was adopted without human verification.

\textbf{Scope and depth of use (chapter-specific).}
The use of AI was concentrated in the \textit{Related Work} part of the thesis process. Two distinct use cases were applied:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Literature review support:} AI assistance was used to generate candidate keyword combinations, suggest potential thematic clusters, and propose initial structuring ideas for the state-of-research narrative.
    \item \textbf{Language polishing (Gemini):} Gemini was used to improve wording quality, readability, and stylistic consistency of selected draft passages.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What AI was not used for.}
AI was not used to fabricate data, generate empirical results, run simulations, derive mathematical proofs, or make final methodological decisions. Model design, optimization formulation, parameterization, implementation logic, interpretation of findings, and all final scientific claims remain the sole responsibility of the author.

\textbf{Quality assurance and validation procedure.}
All AI-assisted content underwent manual verification before inclusion:
\begin{itemize}
    \item bibliographic candidates were checked against original sources;
    \item theoretical statements were cross-checked with cited literature;
    \item mathematical notation and equations were reviewed for consistency with the model implementation and nomenclature;
    \item edited text was revised to preserve intended meaning and thesis-specific terminology.
\end{itemize}
Only content passing this review process was integrated into the manuscript.

\textbf{Authorship and accountability.}
The intellectual contribution, argumentation structure, critical assessment of prior work, and final written version were produced and approved by the author. AI tools served as assistive instruments comparable to advanced editorial support. Responsibility for accuracy, completeness, citation integrity, and compliance with university regulations lies fully with the author.

\textbf{Summary declaration.}
AI support was limited, transparent, and chapter-focused (primarily Related Work), with Gemini used for text polishing and AI-assisted prompts used for literature review scaffolding. The scientific substance, methodology, and conclusions are independent author contributions.
